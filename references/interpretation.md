# Interpretation protocol

## Order of judgment

1. Identify the day master and month-command seasonal qi.
2. Check visible stems, branch roots, hidden stems, support, drainage, control, combinations, clashes, and whether any transformation has sufficient conditions.
3. Classify day-master strength as a reasoned range, not a binary fact when evidence is mixed.
4. Evaluate ordinary structure before considering special or following structures. Require strong evidence for special structures.
5. Discuss useful and favorable elements as hypotheses tied to a named school: balancing, climate adjustment, structure, or disease-remedy. Explain when schools may disagree.
6. Interpret ten gods contextually by position, strength, and interaction. Never map a single ten god directly to a guaranteed event.

## Luck cycles

Confirm sex, year-stem yin/yang, direction convention, solar-term interval, and start-age calculation. Show the rule used. If software supplies the cycles, transcribe its settings before interpretation. Treat annual pillars as themes interacting with the natal chart and luck cycle, not isolated predictions.

## Communication

- Use “可能、倾向、在……条件下更明显” rather than certainty.
- Give balanced manifestations and practical, low-risk suggestions.
- Avoid moral labels such as “克夫、命硬、灾星”. Translate traditional shorthand into neutral language.
- For compatibility, analyze communication patterns and value conflicts; do not declare that two people must marry, separate, cheat, or harm each other.
- End with a concise note that BaZi is a traditional cultural framework without scientific validation.
